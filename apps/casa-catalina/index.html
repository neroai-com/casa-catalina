Good to know

Questions, answered

Is the golf cart really included?
Yes — a complimentary street-legal golf cart comes with the home for your entire stay, parked on-site. It's the perfect way to explore all of Avalon, and guests consistently say it's the highlight of their trip.
How many people does it sleep?
Six. Two bedrooms with king beds — each with its own en-suite bathroom — plus a queen sleeper sofa in the living room.
When can I check in and out?
Self check-in is by lockbox, for a flexible and private arrival. Once you're booked we'll send a welcome message with full check-in instructions by text and email.
How do I book and pay?
Call or text the host directly, or send an inquiry with your dates and guest count. We confirm availability, then take payment by PayPal, Venmo, or Zelle — no booking fees.
How do we get there?
Catalina Express ferries run about an hour from Long Beach, San Pedro, or Dana Point. From the landing it's a short walk up to the home — or ask us about luggage help.
Are pets okay?
We've happily hosted guests with pets — just ask us before you book and we'll do our best to say yes.
